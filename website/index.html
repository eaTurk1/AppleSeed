The first connection

02
AYour Mac +
  1. Install Tailscale, sign in, and connect. Enable MagicDNS and HTTPS for your tailnet if requested.
  2. Open System Settings → General → Sharing → Screen Sharing. Allow the Mac account you’ll connect with.
  3. Unzip the Mac download, put AppleSeed.app in Applications, and open it. In its menu bar, choose Setup Mac service….
  4. Choose Copy Mac address from the AppleSeed menu. This is the private address you’ll enter on your phone.

Keep AppleSeed.app in the same location after setup. If macOS blocks the first launch, use its normal Privacy & Security → Open Anyway flow after reviewing the app.

BYour phone +
  1. Install the APK. Android may ask you to allow installation from your download app.
  2. Connect Tailscale under the same user identity as your Mac.
  3. Open AppleSeed and paste the address copied from the Mac. It looks like https://my-mac.my-tailnet.ts.net:9447.
  4. Connect with the Mac account you allowed in Screen Sharing. Choose Remember on this phone to keep the password encrypted on this device.

You can change the Mac address in Session settings.

CUnlock & use it +

Choose Unlock Mac from the opening screen, or Unlock in the live toolbar. Select your Mac account if needed, then tap the button in the strip below the desktop image. Enable AppleSeed in macOS Accessibility for the secure-field shortcut.

Slide to move the cursor, tap to click, hold and slide to drag, pinch to zoom, and rotate a phone for fullscreen. Tap Full screen beside Disconnect for edge-to-edge viewing. Use Controls to reveal the toolbar, or Exit full screen to return. Tap Apps or swipe up with three fingers to choose an open app. Expand it for windows, or Chrome-family browser tabs. Monitor controls, Keyboard, stream quality and optional timers are included.

Android retains the session when you leave the app. iOS may suspend networking in the background. Network loss, host sleep or process termination can interrupt any client.

DWindows & Linux hosts +

Extract the desktop app and open it. To connect to a Mac, paste its private Tailscale address. To share the Windows or Linux computer, install a password-protected VNC server bound to localhost, then expand Share this computer in AppleSeed.

Linux Wayland support depends on the VNC server and compositor. Mac secure-field Unlock and macOS display settings are Mac-specific. On other hosts, use Keyboard for OS sign-in.

↗Web Portal +

Open Web Portal directly from the opening screen or live toolbar. Enable it for a chosen duration and copy the one-use code. On the other computer, visit your configured /remote URL and enter that code. Two browser windows can show separate monitors.

The public route stays unavailable until you enable it, and Disable revokes access immediately. HTTPS reverse-proxy hosting is separate setup. The owner API and VNC remain private.

When you need to get in

03

An OpenClaw setup, a Codex workstation, or a Mac at home waiting on a permission prompt. AppleSeed gives you the desktop to configure it and approve the prompt yourself.

You use your existing Mac account and permissions. AppleSeed doesn’t bypass passwords or macOS security controls.

Know the limits

04
Locked vs. powered off
A normal locked Mac can be reached while the service and Tailscale are running. Standard setup starts after sign-in. AppleSeed cannot power on an offline Mac or unlock FileVault before macOS starts. Optional boot setup →
Keep it private
Use private Tailscale Serve. Keep VNC and the owner API private. Only the separate authenticated Web Portal listener may be routed publicly. People near the Mac’s monitors can see the session; privacy covers and virtual mobile displays are not included.
Free and open source
Every feature is included under MIT. Optional tips support development and do not change access. The new platform ports are development builds; review the included setup and validation notes.
